Support

Bloomood is a private mood journal. Pick how you feel, add a note if you like, and each day becomes a flower. Your entries stay on your phone.

Common questions

Where are my entries stored?

Only on your phone. Bloomood has no account and no server.

How do I move my entries to a new phone?

Open Settings and choose Export backup, then save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone. On the new phone, open Settings and choose Restore backup.

I forgot my passcode.

A passcode can’t be recovered. On the lock screen, tap “Forgot passcode?” to erase the notebook and start again. If you exported a backup earlier, you can restore it afterwards.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes every entry from your phone. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them.

If you need support now

Bloomood can’t replace professional care. If you are struggling, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines in your country.
