Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

What Bloomood stores

Your check-ins, notes, tags, worries, water records and settings are saved on this phone only. Your PIN is kept in the phone’s secure storage.

What leaves your phone

Your entries never leave this phone unless you export a backup. A backup is a file that you choose where to save or send. Bloomood does not upload it anywhere.

App updates

When Bloomood opens, it asks Expo’s update service (EAS Update) whether a newer version of the app is ready. That request carries the phone’s operating system, the app version and a random token that tells the service whether this installation has asked before. It never includes your entries, your name or your phone’s hardware IDs.

No account, no tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. Bloomood does not collect or share personal data.

Reminders

Check-in and worry reminders are scheduled on your phone and never pass through a server.

Phone backups

On iPhone, depending on your settings, iCloud backups may include Bloomood’s data. Those backups are managed by Apple. On Android, Bloomood is left out of Google backups.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app, or erasing the notebook from the lock screen, removes your entries from this phone. Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy or the app, write to bloomood.app@gmail.com.
